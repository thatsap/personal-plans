## I. WHAT THIS IS

This is not a monologue to read when I am about to break.

This is a **register**.

On July 13, 2026 I burned the shrine: deactivated Instagram, deleted her contact, deleted messages, deleted what I had made for her. Rules: no texting. Instagram only later, not to check on her.

On August 20 I wrote the Kai funeral.

Nine days later, on my birthday, she came back into the thread.

I am logging it so I cannot rewrite Saturday into a reunion, and so I cannot rewrite myself into a villain for still loving her.

## III. THE CHAT — WHAT WAS SAID

### She sent

- *Happy birthday, Ashutosh. Be happy and i hope you are doing well in life. Stay true to yourself and appreciate everything around you.* (Sat, ~6:18 PM)
- Video of her birds (two budgies in a cage).
- *I wouldn't take much of your time but there was something i forgot to give.*
- *Remember she loves you.*

### I sent (the pour)

I hearted her messages.

Then I wrote that I love her and “her” most ardently, more than anything, that no time or delay can change that. I hoped she had moved because remembering bitter things is not good. I said I am hopelessly romantic. I **agreed** that I turn things bitter and cut the only one who ever mattered. I said I am who I am. I said I love her more than anyone ever will. I took it with me “delusionaly Gauri.” I said sorry for saying it. I wrote: **I LOVE YOUUUUU AND ONLYYY YOUUUUUUU.**

Later: *its okay nayan* — she is the sweetest part of a miserable life — I will always look back — the best that ever happened — the one who heard me — entire existence and heart to devote — hopelessly in love, sorry — I love you — pursue your dream — that is also my dream — *you are my dream* — *pyaarii* — I am always here — I read her pins — yearner — Frost: *promises to keep and miles to go before I sleep.* I sent the Heathcliff line from *Wuthering Heights*.

I stalked. I agree. I had not acted on it before this thread. This thread is where I acted.

### She replied

- *I don't know how to react on that.*
- She had wanted to say something yesterday and didn’t know whether to say it.
- *Whenever you will need me, i will be there for you. That's it.*
- *Take it as a gesture of years of friendship we hold Or something deeper than that but yeah i will be there*
- *Wuthering Heights* talk: she watched it, loved it.
- *So you ready to be my friend?*
- *Yes you did But floating alphabet cannot be taken as permanent as craving one*
- Hearts on many of my messages.
- **Nickname set to `.`** — a period. A full stop. Not my name.

She never said *I love you too.*

I never asked for her friendship. She offered it after I bled.

## VI. MORAL REGISTER

Logged so I stop retrying this in my head:

- **Loving her with every bone is not a crime.** Unreturned love is tragedy, not guilt.
- **Still loving her is not wrong.** She does not have to sign it.
- **Hurting is not evidence I failed.**
- **She is not wrong for not loving me.** Nobody owes their heart. She is allowed to be done. She is allowed to have a bf, a best friend, a crush, comment-section flirting. That can gut me and still not be a moral crime.
- **She is not a saint.** She knocked. She knocked again. After I bled she said *I’ll be there*, *be my friend*, *or something deeper* — words that sound like a home and cost her almost nothing. I did not ask for that door. **That is careless.** Careless is not evil. The cost landed on me.
- **Passion: right. Not loving me back: allowed. Birthday + birds + unsolicited friendship while she lives another life in public: thoughtless toward me.** Stalking comments afterward is not a sin against her; it is me reopening my own wound.

There is no courtroom where I win her back by being more moral. I was already the one who loved.

## VII. WILL SHE COME BACK

**No.** Another text from her is not her coming back.

She already texted first. Then she texted again. She used that to offer friendship and a period for a name. She did not say she wants me.

If she writes again unprompted it will almost certainly be the same currency: light, warm, bounded, leftover kindness. My chest will call it a return. It is not. A return looks like *I want you.* Not *be my friend.*

She might message again. That still isn’t coming back.

## VIII. STANDING ORDERS — THE DEED

**I will not text her.** Not first. Not for why. Not for `.`. Not about the comments. Not to apologize for Saturday. Not for closure.

*(Broken the same afternoon. Closure ask logged in [AUGUST-31-2026-CLOSURE.md](AUGUST-31-2026-CLOSURE.md). New order: do not buy the no a second time.)*

### If she texts again

1. **Do not reply in the spike.** Phone down. Wait until the next day if I can.
2. Read what she **sent**, not what I hope it means.
3. **Light message** (hi, meme, how are you, friendship, birds, festival): no pour, no I love you, no *pyaarii*, no poetry, no cathedral. Either **no reply** (allowed) or **one short line** — *Hope you’re well.* — then stop.
4. **If she asks to be friends again:** I am still in love. That room keeps the knife in. I can say: *I can’t do friendship right now.* I do not owe a yes I did not ask for.
5. **If it is actually a return** — she says she wants *me*, not a friend: still wait a day. Then answer like an adult, not like Saturday. *I’ll be there* is not that. I have already seen that sentence.

**Default:** another text is not an emergency and not a reunion. I already gave the whole heart once. I do not donate it again to a maybe.

Mute / unfollow / get the live feed out of my hands is first aid, not punishment. I have punished enough. Occupied mind does not clear by thinking harder. Body and work for the hour. Survive the occupancy until it gets quieter.

I can still love her and still not go back to the chat. Those two can sit in the same chest.
