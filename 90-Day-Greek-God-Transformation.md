## NUTRITIONAL PROTOCOL (THE FUEL)

### Macronutrient Strategy

#### Daily Macro Breakdown

| Macronutrient | Grams | Calories | % of Total |
|---------------|-------|----------|------------|
| **Protein**   | 220g  | 880 kcal | 36%        |
| **Carbs**     | 250g  | 1000 kcal| 41%        |
| **Fats**      | 65g   | 585 kcal | 23%        |
| **TOTAL**     | -     | **2,455 kcal** | 100%   |

#### Scientific Reasoning

**High Protein (2.3g per kg body weight):**
- Maximizes muscle protein synthesis
- Increases thermogenesis (burns 25-30% of protein calories during digestion)
- Preserves lean mass during caloric deficit
- Vegetarian sources require higher intake due to varied amino acid profiles

**Moderate Carbohydrates (strategically timed):**
- Fuels high-intensity training sessions
- Powers badminton performance (explosive movements require glycogen)
- Timed around workouts to maximize glycogen replenishment
- Supports athletic output and recovery

**Moderate-Low Fat:**
- Essential for hormone production (testosterone, growth hormone)
- Supports vitamin absorption (A, D, E, K)
- Keeps calories controlled for aggressive fat loss
- Prioritizes omega-3s and monounsaturated fats for inflammation control

### Progressive Overload Strategy

#### **WEEKS 1-4: Adaptation Phase (Foundation)**

**Goal:** Master form, establish baseline strength, neurological adaptation

**Progression Rules:**
- If you hit the **top of the rep range** (e.g., 8 reps on a 6-8 scheme) for ALL sets with RPE 8-9, add **2.5kg** next week
- If you can't hit the **bottom of the rep range**, maintain weight and focus on rep quality
- **No failure** - leave 1-2 reps in the tank (RPE 8-9)
- Focus: Mind-muscle connection, tempo adherence

**Example:**
- Week 1: Bench Press 80kg x 4 sets x 6 reps (RPE 8)
- Week 2: Bench Press 80kg x 4 sets x 7 reps (RPE 8.5)
- Week 3: Bench Press 80kg x 4 sets x 8 reps (RPE 9)
- Week 4: Bench Press 82.5kg x 4 sets x 6 reps (RPE 8) ✅

---

#### **WEEKS 5-8: Growth Phase (Intensification)**

**Goal:** Maximize muscle hypertrophy, increase training volume

**Progression Rules:**
- **Compound Lifts:** Add 2.5-5kg when top rep range is hit for all sets
- **Accessory Movements:** Add 1-2 reps before increasing weight
- Introduce **drop sets** on final sets of isolation exercises (reduce weight by 30%, perform to failure)
- Push to RPE 9-10 on compound lifts
- Add **rest-pause sets** occasionally (perform set to failure, rest 20 seconds, perform another mini-set)

**Volume Adjustments:**
- Monday PUSH A: Add 1 set to compound movements (5 sets instead of 4)
- Tuesday PULL A: Increase deadlift to 5 sets
- Consider adding a 10th exercise to arm days

---

#### **WEEKS 9-12: Peak Phase (Maximum Intensity)**

**Goal:** Peak strength and conditioning, final fat loss push

**Progression Rules:**
- Push intensity to **RPE 9-10** on all compound lifts
- Implement **cluster sets** on main lifts (perform 3 reps, rest 20 sec, repeat x 5 for same load)
- Use **mechanical drop sets** on final sets (e.g., incline press → flat press → decline press without rest)
- Add **forced reps** with spotter on final sets (1-2 reps past failure)

**Week 12: Deload Week**
- Reduce weight by **40%** on all exercises
- Maintain rep ranges and sets
- Focus on recovery, form refinement, and mental reset
- Prepare body for post-transformation maintenance phase
